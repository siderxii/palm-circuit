FastLoop Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short: your fasting data stays on your phone. FastLoop has no account, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell data. The only information that leaves your device is what's needed to process an in-app purchase.

This policy explains how the FastLoop app for Android ("FastLoop", "the app", "we") handles information. FastLoop is published by palm circuit.

Information stored on your device

FastLoop stores the following only on your phone:

  • Your fasts: start and end times, the goal and the plan you chose.
  • Your settings, such as alert preferences and your default plan.
  • Whether Premium is unlocked, so it keeps working offline.

We don't operate servers for this data and can't see it. Fasting times can be health-related information, and they are never sent to us or to any third party.

Information we don't collect

FastLoop does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or advertising ID. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

In-app purchases

FastLoop Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and unlock Premium on your devices. For this, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app;
  • purchase details, such as the product, transaction and purchase token;
  • basic technical information sent with any network request, such as app version, Android version, country or currency, and IP address.

Your fasting history is never sent to RevenueCat or Google. Google and RevenueCat handle this information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Notifications and alarms

Fast alerts are scheduled and shown entirely on your device. FastLoop asks for these permissions:

  • Notifications: to show the "1 hour to go" and "Goal reached" alerts.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional): so alerts arrive at the exact time.
  • Run at startup: to restore your alerts after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and Google Play billing: only for purchases.

You can change these permissions at any time in Android settings.

Device backups

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include FastLoop's data in your device's backup to your Google account, so your history can be restored on a new phone. Google manages these backups under your account settings; we can't access them.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a single fast by swiping it away in History.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling FastLoop, or in Android Settings > Apps > FastLoop > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as their policies and the law require. To ask for the RevenueCat record linked to your purchase to be deleted, contact us.

Children

FastLoop is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

FastLoop is a general wellness tracker. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor before starting a fasting routine.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
